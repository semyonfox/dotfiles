# Conference trip shortlisting notes

Use this when the user asks broadly for "any conferences" or "what's possible" within a travel budget rather than pricing one named event.

## What worked
- Treat the task as event discovery + budget feasibility, not just flight pricing.
- Start with nearby/cheap-access geographies from Ireland: Dublin/local, Edinburgh/UK, Prague/Bucharest/Copenhagen/Lisbon/Amsterdam/Berlin depending on the event's ticket price.
- Prioritize total trip cost, not event prestige. A cheap/free ticket plus direct low-cost flight can beat a famous event with a €1k ticket.
- Include at least one sightseeing day in the itinerary shape: usually arrive the day before, attend event, sightseeing day, return after.
- Separate "free/cheap entrance" from "travel is cheap". Expensive events like Web Summit can blow the budget before flights.

## High-signal examples from the June 2026 session
- DIGIT Expo Edinburgh 2026: free ticket, Shannon/Dublin to Edinburgh direct can be extremely cheap; excellent candidate for a €300-€500 short trip.
- How to Web Bucharest 2026: startup/tech conference with low public tickets (e.g. Discover/Builder tiers around €99-€129) and cheap local accommodation; good serious-conference option under €1k.
- Turing Fest Edinburgh 2026: potentially good founder/investor room and cheap Ireland-Edinburgh flights, but access is curated and ticket price may not be visible until approved.
- TechBBQ Copenhagen 2026: strong startup event with a startup pass around €229, but Copenhagen accommodation/food pushes all-in closer to the top of a €600-€1k band.
- Web Summit/Slush/large flagship events: often fail this budget because ticket and accommodation dominate even when flights are reasonable.

## Answer shape
1. Start by correcting scope if needed: "not SF/YC specifically, broad cheap conference trips".
2. Give a compact ranked table with event, city/date, entrance, flight evidence, and rough all-in.
3. Call out the best cheap trip and the best serious-value trip separately.
4. Flag "cheap but cursed" routes, hidden ticket uncertainty, or expensive-city accommodation.

## Pitfalls
- Do not overfit to the previously discussed event if the user says "no, other conferences".
- Do not list famous events without ticket prices; admission is often the budget killer.
- Do not treat Knock as preferable just because it is geographically close; Shannon/Dublin often have cleaner direct routes.
- Avoid academic conference-alert spam unless the user explicitly wants paper/research conferences; startup/dev/community events are usually more useful for this user.
