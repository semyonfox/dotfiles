# Learning profile prompt

Paste this into any AI that has memory or past-chat search. Paste the answers
back into a study session and ask for `Learning profile.md` to be updated. A
line needs an `[applied]` point, or the same point from two sources. Ignore
counters like "(2/6)" or "2/3": they are message-split markers, not answers.

```
I'm building a profile of how I learn so a study tutor can teach me the way
that works for me. Use your memory and search our past conversations.

Only use conversations where you explained or taught me something about my
studies, coding or technical work. Skip cooking, drinks, shopping, admin,
deadlines and grades, unless they show how an explanation landed.

Tag every point with one of:
[applied]   I used the idea correctly afterwards, on a new case, without help
[explained] you explained it but there's no later evidence I could use it
[guess]     your inference

Give a short quote or paraphrase from a real conversation for each point.
Leave out anything personal that isn't about how I learn. If a section has no
evidence, say so instead of filling it.

## Clicked
Times I was confused and then got it. What move worked: an analogy (to
what?), a worked example, a diagram, a smaller step, code, maths, a story?

## Didn't land
Explanations I pushed back on, asked you to redo, or still got wrong after.
What was wrong with them?

## Anchors
Tools, languages, projects and hobbies I clearly know well enough to build
analogies on.

## Recurring mistakes
Kinds of mistakes I repeat across topics, not one-off misses.

## Delivery
Length, pace, tone, try first or example first, when I want a summary. Only
what my behaviour shows, not just what I said I prefer.
```
