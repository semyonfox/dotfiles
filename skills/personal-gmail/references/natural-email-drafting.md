# Natural Gmail draft style for Semyon

Use this when drafting or updating replies in Semyon's Gmail.

## Style defaults

- Keep it short, warm, and specific.
- Avoid em dashes.
- Avoid semicolon joins used as polished connector glue, e.g. `The session was useful; it helped...`.
- Avoid generic AI-polish phrases like `genuinely useful`, `I would be delighted`, `valuable insights`, `I hope this email finds you well`.
- Prefer plain transitions: `I read through them properly`, `that helped me think more clearly`, `once I have something live`.
- If the product/project is pre-launch, say that plainly. Do not imply traction, customers, users, or launch status that does not exist.
- For networking follow-ups, do not over-explain the project. Mention the relevant angle and leave the next step light.

## Drafting workflow

1. Read the full thread and preserve reply context.
2. If Semyon gives extra context from LinkedIn/Discord/notes, fold it into the email before finalizing.
3. If the email references a talk, slides, or notes, skim the source material first where accessible and make the reply relevant to the actual content.
4. Create or update a Gmail draft only. Do not send unless Semyon explicitly says to send.
5. Verify the draft exists and check Sent for matching accidental sends.

## Example shape

```text
Hi Name,

Thanks again for sending over the slides and notes from the talk. I read through them properly, and they helped me think more clearly about what I should measure from the start instead of trying to retrofit the data later.

The product has not launched yet, so I do not have real user data to work from, but I have a much better idea now of how to set up the early funnel: traffic source, first useful action, activation, and where people drop off.

Once I have something live and a bit of early signal, I would definitely value your perspective.

Best,
Semyon
```
