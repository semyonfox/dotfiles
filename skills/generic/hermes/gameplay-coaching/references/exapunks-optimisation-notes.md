# EXAPUNKS Optimisation Notes

## Safe hint boundary

When a player asks for hints only, point them to a trace, dependency, or controlled experiment. Do not state the final instruction sequence or replace their code.

## Timing first

In multi-EXA solutions, repeated `LINK`, movement, or apparent idle work can be intentional pacing. Before calling it redundant, trace both EXAs by cycle and note when each global-memory hand-off becomes available or blocks.

## Metric discipline

Treat metric meanings as game-specific. Confirm them from the in-game UI/manual, a trustworthy reference, or a demonstrated before/after test. Do not infer an activity score from a superficial count of operations.

## Response after a correction

Say the prior read was wrong, identify the real dependency in one sentence, and give a revised nudge rather than escalating into a solution.
