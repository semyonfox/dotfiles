full-send ship-changes (partly) Takes a change all the way to a merged PR while you're away: it proves the change, gets it reviewed, files it, babysits it and merges it. It's allowed to merge, force-push its own branch and skip ask-first testing rules. ship-changes only runs the stages you name, sorts each change into a risk tier and leaves risky work as a draft. AI attribution: my take

Don't drop it. Make it off by default and turn it on per repo.

Right now there's a global ban, and five of these skills add attribution: the file-pr footer, the council score table, the sol-loop line, the leaving-pr-comments banner and the takeover note. Trying to remember to strip it from university repos is the wrong way round. One missed repo and it shows up in coursework.

  • Keep the global rule as "no attribution unless the project says so". If someone forgets, nothing leaks.
  • Opt in with one line in a project's AGENTS.md, for example attribution: on, in your own open-source and work repos.
  • The skills check that line, so they can keep their footers and banners without a second "uni" procedure.

One caveat: a git trailer and your university's AI-use policy are different things. Some modules require you to disclose AI use. Hiding it from commits doesn't cover that, so check the policy for each module.
