# International work-placement search notes

Use when Semyon is looking for a university work placement/internship and wants more than a safe local option.

## Durable preference / framing

Semyon may reject a generic local-placement shortlist as too safe. For placement searches, distinguish two goals early:

1. **Module compliance** — the role must satisfy University of Galway placement requirements.
2. **Life/pivot value** — prestige, city, learning rate, story, and career signal matter.

If he asks for “big names”, “San Fran”, “France”, “life experience”, or says standard local jobs are not enough, do not keep pitching normal Galway/Irish employers as the main lane. Keep them as safety nets, but build an evidence-backed prestige radar.

Avoid Russia and too-eastern Europe for this class of search because Semyon is Russian-born and does not want geopolitical/military-service risk around Russia/Ukraine.

Language comfort for opportunity triage: English ideal; conversational French may be tunable; basic German/Spanish/Portuguese; a few words of Italian.

## Placement constraints to verify first

For University of Galway CS&IT-style placements, verify:

- Required start/end: e.g. January to August/September.
- Whether a 6-month internship satisfies the requirement or the full 8 months is mandatory.
- Whether self-sourced international placements are allowed.
- Whether the employer must sign a placement agreement / convention de stage / insurance paperwork.
- Whether US J-1 Intern placements can be approved.
- Whether split placements are allowed.

The 6-month answer is a major branching point: many prestige roles are 5–6 months, while exact 8–9 month roles are rarer.

## Evidence-first search workflow

Do not stop at aspirational company lists. For each target, find one of:

- current job listing,
- official early-careers page,
- archived/mirrored posting,
- university placement PDF/page,
- student story/blog/LinkedIn evidence,
- recruiter/career-page wording showing co-op, placement, academic-year placement, Jan-start, or 6–12 month support.

Classify each source as:

- **Exact fit evidence** — Jan–Aug or 8-month work placement explicitly shown.
- **Strong near-fit evidence** — 6–12 month, flexible, co-op, placement, or academic-year wording.
- **Prestige but weak fit** — real internships, but mostly 10–16 week/summer only.
- **No useful evidence** — internships may exist, but no public placement-format proof.

Quote the exact wording that proves duration/start/placement compatibility. Semyon responds better to “here is the wording” than broad career advice.

## Prestige targets that had useful evidence in July 2026 research

Evidence-backed target patterns found:

- **Amazon/AWS Dublin** — official SDE internships; historical Dublin listing said “6–11 month Software Development Engineer internships” and “flexible in length to fit in with your university’s placement scheme.” Strong big-name target.
- **Snowflake Berlin** — SWE intern listing with “4 month minimum, 6 months recommended, up to 12 months supported, start date flexible”; note one extracted listing wanted completed BS + MS/PhD, so check eligibility.
- **Datadog Paris** — 6-month SWE internships; excellent SRE/platform fit, but check graduation-year and fixed-start constraints.
- **Cloudflare London/Lisbon** — Jan-start SWE internship evidence and separate 6-month placement evidence; strong personal fit because Semyon already uses Cloudflare Workers/Tunnels/Zero Trust.
- **Google** — official 6-month Winter Application Engineering evidence; not always core SWE and location/eligibility may not fit.
- **Apple UK** — official wording for summer internships or “work placement during the academic year”; SWE/cloud-storage mirror wanted Java/Rust/Go/Python and distributed-systems fundamentals.
- **SAP iXp / ASML** — structurally realistic European 5–6 month internships; filter hard for technical roles.
- **Mistral AI Paris** — strong life/prestige fit; SWE internship evidence required a mandatory GitHub project using Mistral API/SDK and had 4–6 month duration.
- **Bloomberg London / GitLab remote / Stripe** — useful to monitor, but need exact role evidence.

Exact-but-less-adventurous safety evidence found: Genesys Galway, Liberty IT Galway, Cisco Galway, Arm Galway, Rapid7 Galway.

## JD vs CV comparison checklist

When comparing job descriptions to Semyon’s CV, produce a fit matrix with:

- Required documents: CV, cover letter/motivation, transcript, enrollment proof, university agreement, GitHub/portfolio, coding assessment.
- Eligibility: degree year, graduation year, work rights, visa/J-1, language, location, return-to-study requirement.
- Duration/start fit: exact Jan–Aug, negotiable 6 months, too short.
- Technical matches: languages, backend/platform, cloud, infra, databases, AI/RAG, distributed systems.
- Gaps: Kubernetes, C++/Go, observability, concurrency/distributed-systems wording, company-specific proof.
- CV edits: exact lines/phrasing to add, not vague advice.

Recurring CV positioning improvements for prestige platform roles:

- Make Cloudflare usage explicit when applying to Cloudflare.
- Bring homelab/CI/CD/AWS/Postgres/Redis higher for Amazon/Datadog/Snowflake.
- Mention reliability/scalability/production deployment where true.
- For Apple/storage roles, surface RustFS/S3/NFS/Btrfs/backups.
- For Mistral, build a small Mistral-specific project with README and easy test path.

## Tone / output preference

Semyon may react badly to polished corporate “blah blah” or safe lists. Keep outputs punchy, evidence-backed, and ranked. If a role is standard/safe, label it as a safety net, not the dream. If a target is glamorous but non-compliant, say so directly.
